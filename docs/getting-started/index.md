# Getting Started

Four steps from download to a disc image:

1. [**Installation**](installation.md): download the archive for your OS and unpack it.
2. [**Your First Project**](first-project.md): open the courtyard example, find your way around, change something.
3. [**Play, Hardware and Discs**](play-and-export.md): boot the scene in the emulator, on a real PS1, and as a `.bin`/`.cue` disc image.
4. [**The Editor**](../editor/index.md): the rest of the interface, one panel at a time.

Nothing else needs installing. The archive carries the editor, the psxsplash engine builds and pcsx-redux.
